Jinki Lee

Senior AI Engineer · Inference & Performance Optimization · POSCO DX

I build low-latency, high-throughput inference systems on GPU and NPU hardware for production. My work covers the whole pipeline, from preprocessing through model execution to postprocessing, with a focus on async execution, accelerator-aware design and cost efficiency.

At POSCO DX I run real-time vision inference for industrial safety and monitoring in steel plants: TensorRT optimization, Triton serving, and NPU benchmarking and integration. Heterogeneous GPU/NPU deployment cut our infrastructure cost by more than 50%.

Before that I spent seven years at AhnLab, first as a SOC analyst and then building ML for SOC automation, including a security-domain BERT trained from scratch. I wrote 안녕, 트랜스포머 (Hello, Transformer), a Korean book that walks through the transformer architecture from first principles.

Seoul, Korea

MSc Warwick · BSc SKKU
